# Skillpack Alpha

Alpha and research skill focused on **Base** and **Robinhood Chain**.

## When to activate
- “what’s trending on base?”
- “new launches on robinhood”
- “alpha on stock tokens”
- “research this token”
- “show me hot tokens today”

## Capabilities

### Token scanner
- Newest launches (last 1h / 6h / 24h)
- Highest volume tokens
- Highest fee-earning creators
- Tokens with sudden volume spikes

### Stock token focus
- Top traded stock tokens (NVDA, AAPL, etc.)
- 24h volume and price change
- Notable inflows/outflows

### Quick research checklist
When user asks about a specific token:
1. Contract address & chain
2. Launch time & launcher
3. Liquidity & holders
4. 24h volume & fee generation
5. Social mentions (if available)
6. Red flags (honeypot patterns, extreme tax, etc.)

## Output style
Keep reports short and actionable:
- Top 5 trending with one-line reason
- Clear “watch / ignore” recommendation
- Link to Bankr launch page or chart when possible

## Example prompts
- “top 5 new tokens on base in the last 6 hours”
- “any interesting stock token volume today?”
- “research 0x… on robinhood”
- “what’s printing fees right now?”
