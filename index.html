What is SKILLPACK?

A collection of ready-to-install skills for Bankr agents operating on Base and Robinhood Chain.

  • Bridge tokens between Base ↔ Robinhood Chain
  • Fair-launch tokens and earn creator fees
  • Trade tokenized stocks
  • Run automation (DCA, TWAP, limit, stop-loss)
  • Stay self-funding through trading fees
